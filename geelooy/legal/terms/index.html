Terms of Use

Version 1.0 · Effective July 14, 2026

These project-authored terms describe the expected use of Awtsmoos.com. They are a product policy statement and are not legal advice.

Accounts and aliases

A private user account may control one or more public aliases. You are responsible for actions performed through aliases you own and for protecting your login credentials.

Public alias identity does not itself prove authority. Server operations may require a current authenticated session, alias ownership, Heichel permissions, and destination policy.

Canonical content and references

Posts, questions, answers, comments, media, and transformed works retain a canonical origin. References, reposts, quotes, excerpts, and promotions should preserve provenance and must not falsely claim authorship or erase their source.

You should publish only content and media you are permitted to share. Voice notes and video reports remain subject to the same standards as written comments.

Community conduct

  • Do not impersonate another person or use an alias you do not own.
  • Do not harass, threaten, exploit, deceive, or coordinate abuse.
  • Do not upload malware, stolen credentials, or unlawful material.
  • Do not manipulate references or transformations to conceal provenance.
  • Use reports and moderation tools honestly and with relevant context.

Heichel governance and moderation

Heichel owners and authorized administrators may define submission, reference, comment, answer, and review policies. Content may be queued, returned for changes, approved, scheduled, rejected, withdrawn, or removed according to those policies.

Role invitations require acceptance. Ordinary role controls do not transfer Heichel ownership.

Availability and changes

Features may change as the project evolves. Policy changes should receive a new version and effective date. Continued use after a clearly presented update may be subject to the updated policy.
