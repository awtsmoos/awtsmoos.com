# B"H — Series (geelooy/api/social/helper/series/)

Series store: direct and virtual series, post membership, and prateem (details) recovery.

## Files
- `directSeriesStore.js` — direct series storage.
- `directSeriesPrateem.js` — series detail (prateem) records.
- `virtualSeries.js`, `virtualSeriesMeta.js`, `virtualSeriesPosts.js` — virtual series views (meta and posts).
- `seriesPrateemRecovery.js` — recovery of series details after corruption/migration.
- `test/` — `directSeriesPrateem.test.js`, `seriesPrateemRecovery.test.js`.
