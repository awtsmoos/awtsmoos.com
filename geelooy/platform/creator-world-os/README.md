# Creator–World OS

Sixty small contracts connect social objects, semantic discovery, published worlds,
persistent characters, verified replays, cinematic timelines, software capability
reports, tunnel evidence, and release safety without coupling their application runtimes.

## Laws

- Drafts are mutable; publications are immutable.
- Provenance and exact internal coordinates remain visible.
- Assets stay outside arbitrary markup payloads.
- Discovery explains its reasons and preserves chronological alternatives.
- Search pins corpus and vector generations.
- Character identity may be shared; game progression remains namespaced.
- Worlds and replays require deterministic validation.
- Artifact reports distinguish inspected, emulated, simulated, rendered, and unsupported.
- Tunnel replay requires immutable input and environment identity.
- Release proof becomes stale when guarded hashes drift.
