Protected Text — live demo

This page shows a paragraph of Hebrew and a paragraph of English that were never sent to your browser as readable text. They arrived encrypted, were decrypted inside your browser's memory, and are drawn only onto canvas — there is no text in this page for a copy, a search, or a recording to find.

How it works, in plain words

  • The key is never sent. Your browser and the server each make a fresh one-time key, swap only the public halves, and compute the same secret on both sides. The secret itself never crosses the wire.
  • Fresh every time. The keys are new for this visit and thrown away when you leave. A recording of this visit cannot open the next one.
  • A second lock on the letters. The font itself is scrambled fresh for this visit and sent encrypted. The text arrives scrambled too, so even the decrypted words are unreadable without this visit's font.
  • Canvas only. The readable words never enter the page as text — only as pixels on a canvas.

The honest limit: anything on a screen can be read from a screenshot. This design does not stop that. It stops the quiet, bulk kind of theft — recording everything the browser downloads and rebuilding the text from it later.
