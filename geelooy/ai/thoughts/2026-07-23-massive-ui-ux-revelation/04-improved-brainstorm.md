# Improved Brainstorm — One Workspace, Three Modes

## Mobile Sanctuary

Chat owns the viewport. A compact app bar provides navigation, title/status, and automation. Left and right panels become full-height drawers with a shared scrim. The composer remains above the safe area. No miniature desktop columns survive.

## Tablet Bridge

The left region becomes a rail or drawer according to measured space. Automation remains a drawer. The conversation owns the center.

## Desktop Observatory

Three regions may coexist with measured widths. Each panel owns its scroll. The conversation canvas is wide while prose remains capped.

## Interaction laws

- One action opens a panel.
- Escape closes the top transient surface.
- Opening one mobile drawer closes the other.
- Focus enters the drawer and returns to its trigger.
- Background scroll locks only on mobile.
- Conversation selection closes mobile navigation.
- Important state uses text, not color alone.

## Visual laws

- Dark navy foundation, restrained cyan/teal accent, warm primary highlight.
- Surfaces separated by tone and border before shadow.
- Clear typography and line height.
- Small token scale for spacing and radii.
- Motion between 140 and 220ms, with reduced-motion support.

## Priority order

1. App shell and viewport behavior.
2. App bar and panel triggers.
3. Conversations and provider setup.
4. Chat reading surface and messages.
5. Composer and attachments.
6. Automation and settings.
7. Empty, loading, error, and status states.
8. Accessibility and browser evidence.
