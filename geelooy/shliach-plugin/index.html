Awtsmoos Shliach

Reveal what you can build with Awtsmoos. Awtsmoos Shliach (Agent), published by Jacob Kaufer, connects an AI conversation to your authorized Awtsmoos account, applications and tunnel devices.

What it does

  • Discovers authorized devices and reports their connection status.
  • Lists directories and reads files you authorize.
  • Discovers current action schemas before using file writes, commands, browser operations, transfers and other tunnel capabilities.
  • Resolves current server and device instructions and saves factual plans, checklists and mission progress.
  • Discovers cataloged Awtsmoos application operations and performs permitted reads and owner-authorized changes.

Capabilities depend on your account permissions, device settings, granted OAuth scopes and the current server catalog. Some actions can change files or run programs. Review the requested action and returned evidence.

Access and price

Use your own Awtsmoos account and a device you own or are authorized to operate. Sign in through the Awtsmoos OAuth flow; do not paste passwords or access tokens into chat. There is no plugin installation charge or in-plugin checkout. The existing Awtsmoos tunnel backend tracks Peruta credits and usage, may require an available credit balance, and can return purchase or upgrade links. Host subscriptions and separately operated services have their own terms. Do not authorize a purchase or upgrade unless you intend it.
