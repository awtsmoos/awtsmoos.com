Awtsmoos Shliach

Reveal what you can build with Awtsmoos. Awtsmoos Shliach (Agent), published by Jacob Kaufer, connects an AI conversation to authorized Awtsmoos accounts, applications and tunnel devices.

What it does

  • Discovers authorized devices and connection status.
  • Lists directories and reads files you authorize.
  • Discovers current action schemas before file writes, commands, browser operations and transfers.
  • Resolves instructions and saves factual plans, checklists and mission progress.
  • Discovers cataloged Awtsmoos operations and executes owner-authorized actions.

Capabilities depend on permissions, device settings, OAuth scopes and the current server catalog. Some actions can change files or run programs. Review the requested action and returned evidence.

Verified screenshots and temporary links

An authorized tunnel can capture a selected Chrome target, transfer image bytes with SHA-256 checks and publish an approved file as a temporary URL. Anyone with a public artifact link may access it until expiration (30–3600 seconds) or revocation. Public sharing requires a separately authorized tunnel.admin scope and file-specific consent. A stored capture receipt does not mean its image was uploaded into an AI conversation. New devices may require pairing; capabilities vary by native-agent version.

Developer review package

Download Awtsmoos Shliach 0.93.0 plugin ZIP (review package; not proof of public directory approval). It includes skills, the MCP configuration and reviewer documentation. Platform submission and approval are separate requirements.

Access and price

Use your own account and an authorized device. Sign in through OAuth; do not paste credentials into chat. There is no plugin installation fee or in-plugin checkout. The backend tracks Peruta credits and usage, may require available credit balance, and can offer separate purchase or upgrade links. Hosting subscriptions and external services have their own terms. Do not authorize purchases unless you intend them.
