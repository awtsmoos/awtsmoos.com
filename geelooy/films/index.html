About these films

Each film pairs ten original AI-generated cosmic scenes — Hebrew letter particles, divine radiance, abstract light — with a full narration of the maamar’s ideas in the Awtsmoos voice. Names are transliterated, never anglicized: Havayah, Elokim, Yisrael. Produced 2026.
