# Live orchestra and Song Studio enhancements

Press **Start** and open **Live Orchestra — layer instruments** above the keyboard. Your original instrument remains playable; up to **four additional synthesized instrument voices** follow each key you play. Choose **Solo**, **Reed & Strings**, **Epic Orchestra**, or **Percussion & Strings**, or build an arrangement with **Add Instrument Layer**.

Each instrument layer can be enabled or disabled, switched between duduk, erhu, two string sections, brass, frame drum and reed/string blend, transposed by up to two octaves, and independently leveled. **Intensity** raises the relative accompaniment strength. All voices join the same audio recording mix; the app limits simultaneous auxiliary voices to protect mobile performance.

To re-orchestrate an editable piano performance, open **Song Studio**, capture or load a **note-based Song take**, open **Studio Enhancements**, change the same layer settings, and **play the take again**. Its original notes and timing are preserved. The arrangement is also stored locally for the next session.

**Important distinction:** These are original synthesized approximations, not acoustic orchestral samples or a backing track. One melody played on several instruments is a layered performance, not separate contrapuntal orchestration. A finished audio file (WebM/MP4/WAV) does not contain editable note events, so its instruments cannot be independently replaced without extracting or replaying notes. No *Avatar* score or recording is included or recreated automatically.
