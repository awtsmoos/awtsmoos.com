# World instrument sounds

Press **Start** in the piano, open its instrument/sound selector, and choose one of the **World •** sounds. The standard keyboard, MIDI input, volume and recording controls continue to work.

- **World • Duduk (Warm Double Reed)**: breathy reed-like lead; use sustained single notes.
- **World • Erhu (Singing Silk Fiddle)**: brighter bowed-string imitation, with stronger vibrato; use expressive connected notes.
- **World • Ceremonial Frame Drum**: short pitched synthesized percussion strikes; play in a lower register for deeper tones.
- **World • Reed & Silk Ensemble**: an original blended reed-and-bow synth timbre; chords and sustained passages work well.

These sounds are **synthesized approximations**, not licensed recordings of Armenian duduk, Chinese erhu, or a traditional percussion ensemble. The blend is a single playable keyboard sound, not a multi-track arrangement. All tones are original; no music from *Avatar* is reproduced. Use **Record Audio** to capture a performance; any supported microphone or MIDI sources use the usual piano workflow.
