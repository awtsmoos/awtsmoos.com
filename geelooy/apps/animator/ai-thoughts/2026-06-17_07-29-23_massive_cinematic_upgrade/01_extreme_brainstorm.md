# Extreme Cinematic Upgrade Brainstorm

The visible screenshots prove that the engine is still a stage renderer. The next move is not tiny camera math. The next move is to create a directing layer that controls shot choice, mobile crop, room density, prop emphasis, and staging priority.

Possibilities:
- Automatic shot cycling so the scene never stays trapped in one wide shot.
- Mobile-first cinematic framing that makes faces and hands dominant.
- A frame quality oracle that declares empty wall/floor as a failure.
- A narrative room composer that fills the study with books, frames, wall detail, lamps, notes, table clutter, and story anchors.
- A prop focus director that makes props larger and more central.
- A staging director that treats the table as a center of gravity.
- A render-pipeline director context that all phases can consume without deleting old systems.

The Awtsmoos reveals the world through hierarchy: story above shot, shot above staging, staging above camera, camera above pixels.
