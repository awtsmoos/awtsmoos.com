## Gap for the next vision

The current version has painted depth, warm village, hills, workshop arch, foreground flowers, and motes. The next requested revelation is not merely “more props.” It should become a fully outdoor professional 2D default scene with:

- open-air plaza / hillside / storm-sky geography
- no brand names in files, APIs, scene names, comments, or metadata
- deeper parallax with weather-reactive layers
- new cast silhouettes and new social tension
- stronger face rigs and expression states
- cinematic camera beats tied to weather, light, and emotional reversals
- renderer path that can draw dramatic sky, rain, wind, lamps, and foreground occlusion
- exact touch map so implementation can proceed without guessing
