## Facial expression brainstorm

A face should not be a sticker. It should be a weather system.

- Neutral attentive: soft eyes, slight smile, relaxed brows.
- Curious: brow up, head tilt, eyes toward object.
- Thinking: mouth corner, brow pinch, gaze up-left.
- Surprised: widened eyes, raised brows, small open mouth.
- Warm encouragement: cheek lift, smile, soft lower eyelids.
- Confused comedy: one brow up, one brow down, mouth skew.
- Realization: blink, eyes widen, smile blooms.
- Proud: chin lift, confident smile, low-angle shot option.
- Shy: gaze down, small smile, shoulders in.
- Happy bite anticipation: eyes focused on food, mouth ready, cheeks lifted.
- Concerned: inner brows up, mouth small, body forward.
- Relief: exhale mouth, brows relax, smile returns.
