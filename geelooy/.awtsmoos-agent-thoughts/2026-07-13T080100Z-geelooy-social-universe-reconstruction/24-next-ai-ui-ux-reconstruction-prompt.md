## 2. THE NORTH STAR

Geelooy must become one spatial language across Home, Spaces, Create, Mail, Profile, Signals, Search, Apps, About, post editor, Heichel editor, comment threads, deep Heichel routes, login, and registration. A user should always know: where am I, what can I do here, what changed, what is global, what belongs to this object, and how do I go back. The interface should feel less like pages inside a website and more like chambers inside one continuous social universe.

Do not make every surface glow. Darkness is not emptiness; it is contrast. One clear action is better than seven equal buttons.

## 3. THE FUTURISTIC VISUAL LANGUAGE

Layer Two is the Vessel: readable content surfaces with restrained translucency, clean borders, stable contrast, and visible hierarchy. Layer Three is the Revelation: rare iridescent edges, live-state pulses, focus halos, selected-route energy, and meaningful motion. The Revelation layer must never overpower text or become the default state.

Create a token system for background depth, readable surfaces, elevated surfaces, text hierarchy, semantic states, spectral accents, borders, focus, shadows, blur, spacing, radii, measure, z-index, motion duration, easing, touch size, shell dimensions, content widths, and safe-area offsets. Establish explicit tokens for micro-labels, metadata, control labels, card titles, page titles, reading titles, long-form prose, numbers, code, Hebrew, and mixed-direction text. Keep long-form reading calm; do not force the theatrical display language into Torah text, messages, comments, forms, or dense settings.

## 7. MOTION AS EXPLANATION
