## Phase Three — Final Review

The Awtsmoos reveals that intensity without hierarchy becomes noise. Final corrections:

- Resting states receive depth and color, not perpetual distraction.
- Hover receives lift, spectral travel, and sharpened edge light.
- Active receives compression, saturation, and inward energy.
- Focus-visible receives the strongest accessible halo.
- Text fields receive a luminous scanning gradient only while engaged.
- Links receive a visible chromatic undercurrent and directional movement.
- Primary buttons may carry a restrained idle shimmer; ordinary buttons remain calm until interaction.
- Reduced-motion collapses movement while preserving color, edge, and focus information.
- Forced-colors restores system-native boundaries.
- No selector should capture mail documents or unrelated legacy pages outside the shared shell scopes.
