## Phase Two — Improvements Over the First Vision

1. Avoid universal pseudo-elements on replaced controls.
2. Use `background-size` motion for buttons and fields.
3. Keep hover effects behind `@media (hover: hover)`.
4. Give active states immediate tactile compression.
5. Keep focus-visible stronger than hover.
6. Preserve disabled clarity.
7. Preserve text selection and caret visibility.
8. Avoid constant animation on every resting control.
9. Animate only interaction or a subtle primary-button pulse.
10. Give links a visible resting identity, not invisible browser text.
11. Exclude skip links from decorative transforms.
12. Preserve visited-link readability without leaking history through dramatic changes.
13. Use `color-mix` only with fallbacks already supported by the project.
14. Keep every touched file below 120 lines.
15. Retain native semantics and form behavior.
16. Respect forced-colors.
17. Respect reduced motion.
18. Keep select controls legible.
19. Avoid layout shift on hover.
20. Ensure touch interactions do not depend on hover.
