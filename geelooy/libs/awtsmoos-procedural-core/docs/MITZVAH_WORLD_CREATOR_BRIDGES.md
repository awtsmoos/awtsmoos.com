# MitzvahWorld Creator Bridges

## Layering

Humans, trees, houses, water, textures, and actions are high-level resource methods layered over the universal registry and transaction executor. They do not bypass validation or history.

## Humans

`humans.create({ id })` defaults to the observed `/games/mitzvahWorld/assets/models/player/chossid.glb`. `modelId` explicitly overrides the default.

## Trees and houses

Initial creators preserve exact parameters, stable identity, revisions, and runtime adapter changes. Geometry compilation remains delegated to existing procedural-core or MitzvahWorld adapters.

## Water

`water.create` and `water.createRiver` preserve geometry/material intent and report fallback provenance.

## Actions

`actions.register` stores structured action resources.

## Stability

The bridge contracts are experimental until concrete live-scene adapters cover every resource family.
