			06 / ADVERSARIAL FONT EXTRACTION
Be explicit about limits

			
The session font has no complete original Hebrew letter glyphs, and its fragment glyph IDs are shuffled. Nevertheless, anyone who downloads it can group 24 equivalent aliases through its cmap and recover the 81 geometric outlines. Public source-font matching or OCR can then reconstruct the visible Hebrew.

			
Spaces, punctuation, word lengths, shapes and fragment counts are still observable. This is obfuscation, not encryption, DRM or access control. Native Android long-press selection remains device-specific; accessible copying, niqqud and cantillation require further work.
