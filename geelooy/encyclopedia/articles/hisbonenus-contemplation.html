Hisbonenus (התבוננות) — Contemplation

Core definition

Hisbonenus is the disciplined, meditative contemplation of Divine matters by which love and fear of Havayah are born. Its definition is "the definition of histaklus" — gazing: the soul's extension toward an object through the brain [31668/maamar_020 :: עב׳ 70]. Yet its essential nature, equal in all its forms, is "finding a thing" (התבוננות היא מציאת דבר) [58841/maamar_021 :: עב׳ 154] — not merely learning a concept but locating the reality of it within one's own apprehension.

How the Rebbeim develop it

The classical question: how can love of Havayah be commanded, if love is a feeling? The Maggid answered: "the command is upon the hisbonenus" — one can be commanded to contemplate [31668/maamar_020 :: עב׳ 58]. Our Rebbe pressed further: Chazal say "a person should always learn where his heart desires," so the command is really upon simas lev — placing the heart — which is what brings the hisbonenus into actuality [31668/maamar_020 :: עב׳ 59–60].

There are two kinds of hisbonenus. The first is the hisbonenus of hasagah — apprehension: the masig (apprehender) truly grasps the musag (the apprehended thing), its depth and inwardness. The second is the hisbonenus of the musag alone — where the grasp is only in that the concept is received in the apprehender, i.e., in examples and parables, not the concept itself and certainly not its depth [31668/maamar_020 :: עב׳ 62–66, עב׳ 112]. True hisbonenus is the first: the joining of apprehender and apprehended.

Every hisbonenus, of either kind, necessarily contains simas lev — the placing of the heart/mind — "for simas lev is the essence of hisbonenus and brings its effect" [31668/maamar_020 :: עב׳ 76–78]. In its root, simas lev means that the matter concerns him personally, greatly — whether the good and useful or the bad and harmful. Hisbonenus without this personal stake remains theory; with it, it produces a calibrated middah: "a middah calibrated to that hasagah — it is through simas lev within the hisbonenus" [31668/maamar_020 :: עב׳ 71–72]. The two modes of simas lev — upon the thing itself, and upon the thing as it concerns him personally — the latter producing inner, essential longing and restlessness until attainment [31668/maamar_020 :: summary].

Hisbonenus has many different levels. There is a hisbonenus that arouses love and a hisbonenus that arouses yirah; within love and fear themselves there are many levels, and each level has its particular hisbonenus according to its matter [58841/maamar_021 :: עב׳ 113–118]. The hisbonenusim of love and fear are "graspings of opposites" — like the oppositeness of kiruv (drawing near) and richuk (distancing): love draws near, fear distances [58841/maamar_021 :: עב׳ 120–121]. Even within love: ahava rabbah b'ta'anugim (love in delights, drawn like water) and ahava d'rishpei eish (love of fiery sparks) are utterly divided — water and fire, opposites [58841/maamar_021 :: chunk summary].

Yet "the many levels in the hisbonenus is only in the hisbonenus's matters alone — not in the hisbonenus's body and essential nature: the body and essential nature of the hisbonenus — its nature is equal in all" [58841/maamar_021 :: עב׳ 151–154]. What is that nature? Hisbonenus is metzias davar — finding the thing: "every reality must have its nature, what it is." The contemplation of one level differs from another in its matter, but in its essence it is the same act: locating a reality.

The soul's relation to hisbonenus is through the brain, but the manner of the soul's revelation in it is hispashtus — extension: the soul extending toward the object of contemplation [31668/maamar_020 :: עב׳ 115]. This is why hisbonenus is the engine of avodah: it draws the soul out toward Divinity, and the middah that results — love, fear — follows the measure of the contemplation.

Key distinctions

  • Hisbonenus vs. simas lev: both are powers of the soul but utterly distinct. Hisbonenus is the contemplative act; simas lev is the placing of the heart — the personal concern that is the essence of hisbonenus and brings its effect [31668/maamar_020 :: עב׳ 76–78, עב׳ 115].
  • Hisbonenus of hasagah vs. hisbonenus of the musag: the first grasps the depth and inwardness of the concept itself; the second grasps only examples and parables — two truly distinct modes [31668/maamar_020 :: עב׳ 112].
  • Hisbonenus arousing love vs. arousing fear: opposites — kiruv vs. richuk — each with its own particular contemplation [58841/maamar_021 :: עב׳ 115–121].
  • Levels vs. essence: the matters of contemplation are divided into many levels; the essential nature of hisbonenus — finding the thing — is equal in all [58841/maamar_021 :: עב׳ 151–154].

Related concepts

Simas lev (placing the heart), Hasagah (apprehension), Ahava and Yirah (love and fear), Histaklus (gazing), Daas (knowledge), Torah study as avodah.
