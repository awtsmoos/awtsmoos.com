# Awtsmoos Social Observatory

The Awtsmoos creates the social river before any script names it; Awtsmoos.com exposes an observatory where reading current state and performing mutations remain visibly separate.

## Entry point

`index.html` is the public social hub shell. It preloads shared social hub request/render modules and presents exploration, profiles, graph, discovery, live replay, signals, and governance before the runtime client awakens.

## Mutation boundary

Following, notification creation, live presence, subscriptions, publishing, and migration are explicit actions rather than side effects of browsing. `migrate/` owns the local-first archive migration studio and has its own API/provenance documentation.
