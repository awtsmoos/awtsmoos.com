Poems from the Shliach world

A project can be precise without becoming dry. These original poems carry the same arc as the agent itself: intention, inspection, building and return.

I · The golden bridge

From intention into deed

A whisper became a prompt in the night, The bridge answered back with a river of light. Not “done” at the sentence, not “built” at the plan— The Shliach went searching where real files began. One plank was a question, one cable a test, One tower said “verify what you actually blessed.” And over the gap where assumptions can fall, The Awtsmoos renewed every step of it all.

II · The Aleph gateway

Where code becomes a doorway

An Aleph stood burning where city lights grew, A coder approached it with something to do. He opened no fiction and guessed at no tree; He asked every file what its story might be. Then modules like vessels made order from flame, Each function remembered why into being it came. And when every pathway had proven its claim, The portal stayed open—but nothing stayed same.
