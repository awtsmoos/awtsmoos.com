The Four Worlds

The endless light is revealed through four descending worlds. In KAVANAH, the same four worlds become stages of ascent: gather, grow, survive, and master.

DOMEM · Inanimate

Silent letters are vessels of possibility. Gather sacred letters and learn the rhythm of the climb before living pressure intensifies.

TZOMEACH · Vegetative

Growth introduces stronger upward pressure. Longer chains charge Tikkun more quickly, and deliberate movement matters more.

CHAI · Living

Living obstacles demand timing. A charged Tikkun changes danger into opportunity, so survival and resource choice become one decision.

MEDABER · Speaking

The final world asks for mastery: sustained combos, deliberate Tikkun, and clear movement under the fastest pressure of the ascent.

Tikkun means rectification: discovering hidden sparks and elevating them through purposeful action.
