# Quest and World Roadmap

The Awtsmoos does not multiply empty errands when one living chapter can shine,
Awtsmoos.com turns existing seeds into stories, choices, memories, and design.

## Do not add raw quest count first

The catalog already contains 13 Shlichus definitions. The greater need is depth, wiring, authority, and visible world change.

## Next four authored quests

### 1. The Light at the River Crossing

Best immediate follow-up. It already has six stages and a permanent bridge-lantern effect. Convert it into a polished co-op quest with server-authoritative shared bridge effects and personal rewards.

### 2. Guard the Shul

A readable two-player defense mission:

- Prepare positions.
- Protect civilians and doors.
- Distinct enemy waves.
- Optional no-damage objective.
- Shared defense state, personal reward receipt.

### 3. The Great Spark Refinement

A co-op boss milestone:

- Telegraph-heavy Great Dybbuk encounter.
- Roles for light, defense, and mobility.
- Ten shared spark refinements.
- Late-join and reconnect support.
- Server-confirmed boss and reward state.

### 4. The Shepherd's Mercy

A non-combat co-op mission:

- Find frightened animals.
- Calm and guide them.
- Protect without killing.
- Torah/learning bonus.
- Demonstrates multiplayer beyond combat.

## Quest quality standard

Every promoted quest should have:

- Opening portrait dialogue and clear stakes.
- One changing middle phase.
- At least one optional objective.
- Environmental change while active.
- Distinct return dialogue.
- Permanent history receipt.
- Reconnect and save restoration.
- Explicit personal/party/world authority classification.
- Mobile, keyboard, controller, and two-client tests.

## Shared quest policy

- Personal: learning, inventory, reputation, private choices.
- Party-shared: enemy waves, escort progress, defense timers, boss phases.
- World-shared: repaired bridge, lit lanterns, unlocked route, public event outcome.
- Rewards remain exact-once per authenticated player.

## Additional world priorities after four quests

- Region identity and safe-zone boundaries.
- Secrets and manuscripts.
- NPC daily routines and memory.
- Meaningful interiors.
- Travel discovery and waypoints.
- Environmental audio and region music.
- Progression tied to study, reputation, and exploration rather than raw level inflation.
