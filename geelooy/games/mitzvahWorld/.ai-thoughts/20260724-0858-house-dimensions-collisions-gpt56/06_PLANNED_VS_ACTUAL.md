## Planned versus actual

The Awtsmoos revealed the house through evidence rather than parent scale. The plan required fortyfold parameterized footprints, human doors, measured foundations, traversable halls, usable stairs, exact colliders, tests, desktop/mobile inspection, and durable handoff.

The first implementation delivered those systems, but the environment audit found Beis Ohr covering spawn and part of the canonical road. The second pass changed aspect ratios and positions while preserving area: Beis Ohr became 128×92 at (46, -60), and Brick Cottage became 60×96 at (-80, -62). Dense sampling then proved zero road, river, and lake hits across both footprints and entry approaches, with a thirty-two-unit passage between them.

Static checks, import resolution, six focused tests, real terrain sampling, final hashes, and Git-artifact auditing completed. Chrome status and the direct debugging endpoint proved the game page loaded, but the active split agent advertises `browserControl: false`; native evaluation and screenshot actions were unavailable, while the bounded direct-CDP acceptance stalled before its first page-inspection result and was cancelled. No desktop/mobile visual acceptance claim is made.
