## Phase one: complete possibility map

The Awtsmoos.com house must become an actual traversable building, not a parent-scaled toy. Considered paths include linear scaling, footprint-area expansion, modular wings, courtyard plans, central halls, repeated room bays, stepped foundations, terrain skirts, exterior ramps, multiple stair flights, and immutable human-scale threshold policies.

The chosen interpretation is measurable and testable: every profile's new width×depth must be at least forty times its recorded legacy width×depth. Door width, door height, panel thickness, mezuzah size, and interaction semantics remain unchanged. Geometry grows through profile parameters only.

Observed roots:

- Profiles contain no legacy-vs-expanded evidence.
- Shell and floors derive correctly from profile dimensions.
- Rooms provide only one partition per story, unsuitable for a 40× footprint.
- Stairs use fixed local offsets and a fixed 9.6-unit run.
- Foundation samples only center terrain height, so large footprints need a measured platform/skirt policy.
- Primitive colliders are generated from exact visible definitions, which is the correct contract to preserve.
