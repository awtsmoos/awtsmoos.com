## Discovery graph

The meadow must be treated as one ecological graph rather than three unrelated renderers. Mounting flows from rich-world orchestration into tree, vegetation, and water systems. Placement flows from deterministic seed and terrain sampling through exclusion policies for road, houses, quests, and combat clearings. Rendering flows through shared geometry and materials. Verification flows through scene counts, finite bounds, continuity, clearance, and resource metrics.

## Evidence already observed

- The real app contains dedicated tree, vegetation, river, and water modules.
- `MinimalMeadowTreeSystem.js` is dirty and includes a resilience material change; it must be preserved.
- Prior workers identified silent material loading as a failure mode.
- The app contains explicit road, house, quest, terrain, rich-world mount, and world-system modules that may be read but not written.
- Existing world tests already cover tree core/material resilience and live-mobile visual rescue.

## Unknowns to resolve before code

- Exact mount ownership and replacement semantics.
- Terrain height sampling contract and playable bounds.
- Road centerline/ribbon clearance API.
- House and quest access coordinates.
- Existing tree geometry/material sharing.
- Existing water path, bank, bed, and animation allocation behavior.
- Test runner and supported tiny-runtime object APIs.
