# Verification Plan

- Health transitions: above zero, exactly zero, and below zero.
- Exactly one `player:defeated` event per defeat cycle.
- Damage, cast, movement, jump, target, and action-bar input rejected while defeated.
- One timer and one respawn; health, collision, camera, movement, and targeting restored.
- Deterministic one-, three-, and six-demon simulations.
- Metrics: incoming DPS, active melee slots, active ranged slots, hit overlap, survival time, and player victory.
- Regression: enemy XP, corpse selection, loot, quest events, and distinct-enemy credit.

The finite test is the vessel; the Awtsmoos is not measured by the vessel, yet every executable claim must still be witnessed by deterministic evidence.
