# Second Pass: Critique and Improvements

A naive URL replacement would scatter the new host through every material file and violate the filename-only requirement. A naive larger grass repeat would make the texture even smaller, because repeat count and visible texel scale are inversely related. A naive selection debounce would not distinguish selecting from interacting. A naive stair fix that always chooses the lower tread would break ascent. A naive door offset would repair one profile while rotating the error into every other yaw.

## Improvements

1. Discover every current remote texture literal.
2. Centralize base roots in one transport module.
3. Store decoded filenames, never encoded URLs, in catalogs.
4. Resolve URL encoding only at the transport boundary.
5. Define semantic ground roles rather than direct filenames inside shaders.
6. Lower grass repeat counts to enlarge visible texture features.
7. Blend dirt-grass at transition zones.
8. Blend cobblestone at road center and dirt at road shoulders.
9. Keep analytic road masks.
10. Add catalog completeness evidence.
11. Trace canonical pointer selection owner.
12. Keep first-click selection side-effect free.
13. Require same-target second click for interaction.
14. Expose selection stage in diagnostics.
15. Reset stage when target changes.
16. Trace hostile damage-to-aggro path.
17. Test every demon profile, not one exemplar.
18. Make retaliation profile-independent.
19. Trace stair height candidates with previous/current vertical values.
20. Include vertical velocity or descent intent in support choice.
21. Allow upper-floor support to yield over stair apertures.
22. Derive door transforms in house-local coordinates once.
23. Test rotated houses.
24. Test story-level doors.
25. Make mobile HUD geometry deterministic.
26. Prevent target cards from growing beyond available width.
27. Use a compact selected-target rail in portrait.
28. Keep touch controls within safe lower zones.
29. Avoid new per-frame DOM writes.
30. Run browser geometry proof after all coding.
