# Phase Two — architecture

One session object owns stable role, target persistence, aggro, leash, lost-target timeout, decision staggering, transition history, cooldown, wind-up, impact/cast, recovery, reposition, pursue, and defeat. A pure role policy selects melee or caster once per engagement. Navigation exposes line-of-sight and collision-aware optional hooks without inventing pathfinding. The public combat controller preserves the actor's existing constructor, update, and diagnostics interfaces.
