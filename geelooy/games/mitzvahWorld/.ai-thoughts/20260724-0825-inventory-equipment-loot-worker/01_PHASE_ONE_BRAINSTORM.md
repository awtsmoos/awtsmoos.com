## Unbounded discovery pass

2. Preserve every duplicate quantity rather than overwriting duplicate saved rows.
3. Support multiple legal stacks when an item exceeds its per-stack limit.

5. Make purchases atomic so listeners never witness an intermediate wallet-only state.
6. Reject invalid quantities before mutation.
7. Reconcile equipment after every removal and restoration.
8. Publish equipment state from the same authoritative store snapshot.
9. Remove query-string module identities from this connected boundary.
10. Keep one procedural weapon object while moving between hand and back.

20. Make Bag controls keyboard navigable and restore focus on close.
