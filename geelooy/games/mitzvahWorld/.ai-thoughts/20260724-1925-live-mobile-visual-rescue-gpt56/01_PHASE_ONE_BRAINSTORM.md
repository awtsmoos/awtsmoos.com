The fastest safe path is to correct the publication crash first, then remove replacement conflicts and encode visual invariants in source. The quest NPC must become an isolated canonical GLB actor rather than coexist with or overwrite a separate friendly population. Terrain and road density must be present in UV coordinates themselves so material-repeat support is an enhancement rather than a dependency. House visibility should be stabilized after rich-world mounting by disabling incorrect culling and backface removal only on house surfaces. Demon bootstrap color must respect `material.vertexColors === false`; the textured rich material remains untouched. Weapons must be marked renderable in both bootstrap and rich paths. Custom action bones must compose from captured bind quaternions instead of multiplying previous-frame offsets.

No screenshot is an acceptance authority. Later evidence will inspect feature receipt, rail DOM state, GLB source identities, UV ranges, material images, road geometry, house flags, demon material flags, weapon parents, action bone quaternions, console errors, and deterministic simulation state.
