## Canonical Procedural Alpine Jewish Mountain Village

The images listed in `REFERENCE_MANIFEST.md` are the controlling visual and spatial specification for the Mitzvah World village rebuild. They are not optional mood references. Their master plan, sections, elevations, transitions, architecture, interiors, ecology, hydrology, and representative compositions govern the implementation.

The source images were supplied through the ChatGPT conversation environment. Their `/mnt/data` paths are not directly mounted inside this Mac repository. This directory therefore records their exact filenames, roles, and authority without falsely claiming that binary copies are present. When a transfer path becomes available, preserve the original filenames here.

Acceptance is based on the browser-rendered village from deterministic gameplay cameras. Tests, texture counts, shader compilation, deployment, and performance numbers are supporting evidence only.
