# bh57 Dynamic Moving Block Collision Rewrite

The present mover is a visual stone with late corrective heuristics. It checks whether the player's feet are near the top after overlap, then tries to rescue the state. That is not a platformer solid.

Required revelation:

1. Moving bodies are runtime AABBs, not octree members.
2. Each body owns position, previousPosition, velocity, and halfExtents.
3. The world keeps one runtime list at `olam.dynamicBodies`.
4. A pathBox rejects players outside the whole route before any detailed test.
5. Top contact uses previous feet and current feet versus previous/current top.
6. Side contact resolves by nearest AABB side, then carries the player by mover velocity.
7. Crush uses unresolved/blocking state and invokes reset/damage through the safest available hook.
