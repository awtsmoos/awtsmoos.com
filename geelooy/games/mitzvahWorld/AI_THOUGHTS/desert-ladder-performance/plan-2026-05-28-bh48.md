# bh48 Choppiness + Missing Door Repair

## Findings
- Lava choppiness is likely from the bh47 fragment shader: sine math per pixel across a massive screen-covering plane on mobile.
- `SpikedBallHazard` is already one Mesh and one BufferGeometry, with no child meshes, but we will verify with static checks.
- Door disappearing likely has two causes:
  1. stale legacy loader imports `../../awtsmoosCkidsGames.js` without version,
  2. door was placed high and narrow after rotation; make it lower, larger, and export both `InteractiveDoor` and `SimpleDoor`.

## Changes
- Replace lava ShaderMaterial with a tiny generated CanvasTexture/DataTexture style material and animate only texture offsets.
- Refresh loader imports to bh48.
- Export both InteractiveDoor and SimpleDoor.
- Lower/scale door in level helper and door class.
- Bump level/import cache chain to bh48.

## Verification
- node --check all changed files.
- grep no ShaderMaterial in SpikeField.
- grep SpikedBallHazard has BufferGeometry and no ConeGeometry/no add(child mesh).
- grep level has InteractiveDoor, SpikedBallHazard, MovingPushBlock, missing_bridge_restored.
