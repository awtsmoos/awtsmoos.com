# Ecology and Realtime Realism

The Awtsmoos renews root, branch, leaf, liquid, dust, light, and motion while Awtsmoos.com lets those finite wonders deepen Har HaOhr without burying combat beneath decorative weight.

## Ecology architecture
Ohrfront will adopt shared-core vegetation through five bounded authorities:
- `ChochmahEcologyRecipe`: deterministic ecological intent,
- `TzomayachTreeAuthority`: tree skeleton/species/material manifestation,
- `TzomayachBotanicalAuthority`: smaller botanical systems,
- `NetzachEcologyLod`: shared-core tree LOD/runtime-profile decisions,
- `GevurahEcologyValidation`: generated-data validation before scene insertion.

## Tree usage
Core tree species, skeleton, LOD planning, runtime profiles, growth and trellis-force APIs are used as data/assembly law rather than per-frame regeneration.

High-quality tiers may reveal:
- richer branch skeletons,
- additional leaf/needle detail,
- closer LOD transition distances,
- greater species variation.

Lower tiers preserve recognizable trunks/silhouettes while reducing decorative detail.

## Botanical usage
Phyllotaxis, species catalogs, vascular/environment models and botanical realism are bounded to authored clusters. Thousands of decorative instances never run independent vascular simulations every rendered frame.

## Collision policy
Only sufficiently large authored trunks or obvious structural plants may become collision/tactical objects. Cosmetic stems, leaves and ground vegetation remain visual-only so culling cannot create invisible collision.

## Realtime realism
Shared-core realtime realism will be concentrated into selected marsh, water, impact, energy and environmental sites.

Potential systems:
- liquid optics,
- secondary particles,
- trails,
- persistent liquid artifacts,
- deterministic stepping,
- bounded native node-graph evaluation.

## Performance law
Realtime realism consumes an explicit budget. Under pressure:
1. secondary particles fall first,
2. trail density falls,
3. persistent cosmetic artifacts fall,
4. complex liquid detail simplifies,
5. gameplay readability remains unchanged.

## Determinism
Environmental seeds and stable recipes ensure repeated map generation creates reproducible evidence. Decorative randomness may vary only when intentionally derived from a documented stable seed.

## Visual direction
The ecology should make Har HaOhr feel physically inhabited rather than fantastical clutter: dry grasses, scrub, resilient trees, marsh growth, weathered stone, disturbed earth, smoke/dust traces and restrained luminous phenomena.

Hebrew/Kabbalistic energy remains exceptional. It should not coat every plant with glow or turn realism into constant spectacle.

## Validation
Generated vegetation and realism data must be validated before manifestation. A failed optional ecological object is skipped or replaced with a simpler fallback instead of aborting runtime assembly.

## Diagnostics
The ADVANCED disclosure may expose ecology tier, visible tree count, deferred detail count, realism tier and bounded effect counts. No such values belong permanently in the combat HUD.

## Verification
- deterministic ecology counts for a known seed,
- tree LOD plan changes with distance/profile,
- invalid generated vegetation is rejected safely,
- visual-only plants never create hidden colliders,
- performance pressure reduces secondary realism first,
- low tier retains environmental readability,
- high tier enriches ecology without changing gameplay outcome.
