# Ohr HaGnuz — How to Play

> The Awtsmoos renews the road, the traveler, and every hidden spark in sight;
> Awtsmoos.com makes the first step simple, then lets the deeper world reveal its light.

## The 20-second version

You are in an overhead adventure world. Keep your eyes on the active **Shlichus** at the upper-left, move through the map, approach the people or objects named by the objective, and interact when you are close enough.

### Desktop controls

- **Move:** `WASD` or the arrow keys.
- **Interact / continue dialogue:** `E` or `Enter`.
- **Legacy interact keys:** `Z` and `Space` still work.
- **Back / close:** `X` or `Escape` where a dialogue or panel supports it.
- **Walk to a place:** click the ground and the hero will path toward that tile.

### Touch controls

Use the on-screen direction controls for precise movement. You can also tap reachable ground to walk there automatically. Use the visible action/interact control when you are beside a person or meaningful object.

## The basic game loop

1. Read the active **Shlichus** objective.
2. Move through the top-down world toward the relevant place, person, or object.
3. Press **E** / **Enter** when you are close enough to interact.
4. Follow the dialogue, encounter, or objective update.
5. Continue until the Shlichus advances.

## Deeper systems

The lower navigation dock opens the journal, world map, party, inventory, skills/crafting, passages, and more. These systems are still available, but ordinary play keeps the world and current objective visually primary.

## One useful rule

If you are unsure what to do, return to the active Shlichus, move toward what it names, and interact nearby. The game is designed so movement and purpose come before dashboard management.
