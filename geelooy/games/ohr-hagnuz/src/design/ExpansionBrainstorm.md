# B"H

# Ohr HaGnuz Expansion Brainstorm

The goal is not to copy Pokemon or RuneScape; the goal is to make a single-player Torah-soul RPG with the research, exploration, collection, combat, skilling, and quest depth that makes those games feel infinite.

</Core Pillars>

1. World
- Multi-region open world: village, beist midrash, forest, orchard, river, cave, desert, midbar, shok, migdal, palace, sky gates, hidden sefiros chambers.
 - Travel gates: doors, region edges, portals, skill-locked gates, quest-locked gardens.
- Random events: wild musagim, lost scrolls, singing trees, wandering magiggidim.

2. Collection
- Wild musagim as concept-creatures that can be studied, sweetened, indexed, and used to unlock quests (not captured, but revealed).
- Musag dex with question label, place found, development stage, sweetening date.
- Iron/bronze/silver/gold mastery for each musag.

3. Battle
- Pokemon-style battle screen: two visible sides, bars, EXP, menu, status, animations.
- RuneScape-style skill contribution: chochmah damage, binah defense, daat critical, niggun heal, hitbonenus evade.
- Tempo system: attack, defend, meditate, niggun, source, svara.
- Status: dazed, awe, shattered, fervor, joy, silence, ohr chozer, hitaapachut.
 - Enemy AI: logical opponents choose moves based on player status.

4. Skills
- Skills like RuneScape: Learning, Writing, Niggun, Hitbonenus, Chesed, Gevurah, Tiferet, Netzach, Hod, Yesod, Malchus.
- Each skill has level, exp, nextExp, and perks.
- World actions grant skill EXP: read scroll, collect spark, fixbridge, meditate at tree, sing at river.

5. Items
- Inventory: sparks, scrolls, keys, chests, lamps, tools, forged letters, food, niggun shards.
- Garments: equipment that changes appearance, stats, moves, and battle effects.
- Tools: pen, lamp, kleiznemer, forge hammer, bridge nusach.

6. Quests
- Main quest: gather hidden light through seven regions.
- Region quests: village, beist midrash, forest, river, cave, orchard, forge, ruins.
 - Side quests: NPC memories, skill tutorials, rare musagim, reward garments.
 - Quest chains with prerequisites and stages.

7. NNCs
- Every NPC has unique glyph, story, quest, battle or skill function.
 - NPC story progresses as quests complete.
- Some NPCs are shops, trainers, teleporters, skill masters, secret-path guides.
