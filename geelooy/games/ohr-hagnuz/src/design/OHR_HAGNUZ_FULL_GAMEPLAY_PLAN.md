# Ohr HaGnuz: I Did Not Forget

This pass turns the game from loose systems into a real gameplay spine.

## Beginning
The player starts in the first village with the guide ג. The road east now opens into the Garden of Ungiven Things.

## Middle
The player collects five entrusted gifts: Terumah, First Tithe, Poor Tithe, Second Tithe, and Bikkurim. Each gift has a receiver and an order. The Court of Rightful Receivers teaches that holiness is given, not sold.

## End
The House of Forgetting checks whether the player removed the holy from the house, gave to the Levi, gave to the poor, remembered blessing, shared joy, and restored flavor to fruit.
