# B"H — Batch 2 Manual Level Plan

Batch 2 covers levels 6–10. These are not generated, mirrored, shifted, or templated. Each is manually typed and given its own design reason.

Design arc:

6. Gevurah Red Bridge — first deliberate gap discipline; weapon is after a judged crossing.
7. Chesed River Islands — generous river platforms, teaching recovery after missed jumps.
8. Binah Terrace Tower — vertical thinking with staggered platforms and a tucked hidden Spark.
9. Chochmah Flash Causeway — faster route reading, enemy before weapon, bright upper reward.
10. Kesser Crown Hill — first Batch 2 mastery gate: two enemies, crown climb, stomp-friendly final platform.

Every level includes:

- Unique title/difficulty/theme/description.
- Explicit idea/progression/enemy/powerup/weapon/secret notes.
- Distinct handwritten rows.
- At least one hidden Spark.

Verification remains the same: 50 registry count, unique IDs, unique layouts, batch metadata, platform/spawn/enemy/powerup/weapon presence.
