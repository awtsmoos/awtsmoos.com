Sulam HaSod

Climb the secret ladder through worlds of sparks, keys, gates, and prowling shadows.

How to play
Desktop: ← → or A/D to move, Space/W to jump. Mobile: drag the orb and tap Jump.
Gather coins, take the key, jump on enemies from above, and enter the glowing gate.
