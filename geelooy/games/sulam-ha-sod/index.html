B"H · Living Kabbalistic Dream Platformer

Sulam HaSod

Lightning-fast chaos: cursed platforms, spike prophecy, brutal traps, Hebrew shatter, and a ladder that keeps getting meaner.

How to play
Desktop: ← → or A/D to move, Space/W to jump.
Mobile: hold Left or Right, then tap Jump. The mobile hit zones extend upward, so nearby taps still count.
You must collect every real coin and the key before the door opens. Fake coins still bite.
