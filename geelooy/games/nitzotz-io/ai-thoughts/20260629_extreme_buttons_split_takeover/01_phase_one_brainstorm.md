# Phase One — Extreme UI Brainstorm

The visible wound: the game technically starts, but the player is trapped inside a confusing ritual of tiny buttons, unclear navigation, and a visual field that does not scream “play me now.” The requested cure is not a small style tweak. It is a usability and intensity takeover.

Possibilities opened:

1. One dominant BEGIN button; everything else becomes secondary.
2. Quality buttons renamed as Smooth, Balanced, Extreme so the player chooses a feeling, not a benchmark.
3. Haptics and PostFX become clear ON/OFF chips.
4. Movement help is embedded directly in the overlay.
5. Touch controls receive labels and huge hit targets.
6. Keyboard controls gain Enter, R, Space, N.
7. HUD becomes sharper and less cryptic.
8. Minimap becomes readable and separated from the action.
9. Gameplay gets real danger: objects too large to absorb can punish reckless contact.
10. Extreme mode tightens time and target.
11. Player feedback becomes immediate: collision warnings, pulse text, stronger messages.
12. One-line JS modules become split readable vessels.
13. The code becomes a palace of small chambers, each with JSDoc commentary.

The Awtsmoos spark in this plan: the buttons are not decoration. They are gates. A gate must be obvious even to a half-awake traveler fleeing chaos through the Four Worlds.
