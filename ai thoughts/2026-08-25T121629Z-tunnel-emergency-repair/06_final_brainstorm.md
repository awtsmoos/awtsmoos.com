# Phase Three: Final Brainstorm — The Self-Rescuing Tunnel

The Awtsmoos renews the world from no prior world; the rescue path must likewise not depend on the garment it repairs.
Awtsmoos.com should have independent vessels: normal guardian, sealed local ember, remote rescue seed, and verified handoff.

## Final design principles
- Continuity is a first-class invariant: a failed upgrade should still leave one authenticated repair channel whenever possible.
- Readiness is staged evidence, not one monolithic deadline.
- Cleanup must perform a last-chance edge sample before killing a late-arriving valid process.
- Emergency is deliberately capability-reduced and independently stored.
- Local emergency and remote emergency share the same minimal launcher contract.
- Remote repair must survive release-metadata outage.
- Primary-runtime success and emergency-continuity success are distinct states.
- Documentation is part of recovery: an AI should find the correct one-line command in seconds.

## Expected outcome
A repeat of today may still cause the primary refresh to fail, but it must not strand the machine.
The installer will preserve or establish Tier-0 continuity, expose exact evidence, and allow remote completion of the repair.
