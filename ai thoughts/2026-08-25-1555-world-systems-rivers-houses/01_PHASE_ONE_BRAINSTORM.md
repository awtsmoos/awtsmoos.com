# Phase One — World Systems Brainstorm

The Awtsmoos renews river beside road and roof beside tree; Awtsmoos.com should reveal worlds from compatible laws, not isolated generators that accidentally overlap.

## Competing directions

### A — River world evidence authority
Canonical centerline plus width, depth, banks, riparian/floodplain influence, crossing evidence, and stable semantic frames. This deepens existing river morphology/flow without duplicating simulation.

### B — Generic environmental field graph
A broad graph for moisture, slope, water proximity, exposure, geology, settlement clearance, and vegetation suitability. Powerful, but too broad as the first implementation slice.

### C — Settlement-only water adapter
Teach villages/buildings to avoid water directly. Small, but it creates consumers that understand river internals and does not produce reusable world evidence.

### D — Geometry-first river meshes
Generate water and bank meshes immediately. Visually direct, but violates the existing renderer-neutral split and risks a second river authority.

### E — Giant world orchestrator
Combine terrain, rivers, roads, houses, rocks, trees, vegetation, creatures, and materials at once. Long-term direction, but premature before domains publish compatible evidence.

## Preferred first architecture

Use A as the foundation. Keep flow runtime authoritative for motion. Add immutable river world-space evidence, then adapters for habitat and village constraints. Extend architecture through its existing profile/shell authority and preserve flat roofs as the legacy default while adding gable/hip/shed grammar. Add non-channel water as separate basin plans for pond/lake/wetland spatial evidence.

## Possible future work unlocked

- canonical river LOD sampling from one path;
- banks, beds, floodplains, riparian habitat, fords, bridges, docks, reeds, driftwood, and navigation anchors;
- lake/pond/wetland shorelines and habitat transitions;
- terrain drainage and river carving intent;
- road/bridge intersection planning;
- flood-aware village and building sites;
- river-responsive vegetation and rock fields;
- watershed/confluence graphs;
- richer roof/opening/facade grammars;
- unified deterministic world recipes with named random substreams.

## Verification targets

Same seed and structural options must produce equivalent spatial evidence. Render quality must not perturb river/basin shape. Legacy water runtime and default flat buildings must remain compatible. New manual roof solids must be finite and structurally closed enough for neutral consumers to triangulate.
