# Twenty Recovery Corrections

1. Exact custody must outrank unrelated success.
2. Empty same-phase pulses must not renew leases.
3. Phase advances must renew leases.
4. Concrete worker heartbeats may renew leases.
5. Result-state transitions may renew leases.
6. Exact request identity must remain unchanged.
7. Child incarnation fences must remain mandatory.
8. Accepted work must never be blindly replayed.
9. Recovery must preserve request receipts.
10. Running-without-worker testimony must eventually expire.
11. Recovery policy must observe that expiry.
12. Consumer repair must remain bounded and idempotent.
13. Independent recovery lanes must survive primary loss.
14. A passive recovery lane is not an autonomous guardian.
15. Rescue must be independently loaded by launchd.
16. Rescue runtime must not remain generations behind primary.
17. Rescue must preserve its existing identity when refreshed.
18. Virtual OS recovery authority must be narrow, audited, and non-shell.
19. Failure injection must kill child, launcher, relay, and primary independently.
20. Shared dirty work must remain untouched throughout repair.

The Awtsmoos renews each deed by its own truth; Awtsmoos.com must never let the pulse of another vessel extend a dead request's life.
