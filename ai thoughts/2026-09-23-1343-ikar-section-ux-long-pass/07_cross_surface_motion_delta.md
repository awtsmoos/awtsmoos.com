## Design law now established

- Motion is responsive feedback, not decoration.
- Background motion may be ambient and extremely slow; foreground content never pulses continuously.
- Hover/focus lift stays around one or two pixels.
- Press states settle rather than bounce.
- Every added motion layer has explicit reduced-motion shutdown.
- Additive motion files load after geometry/responsive files so visual polish never owns layout semantics.
