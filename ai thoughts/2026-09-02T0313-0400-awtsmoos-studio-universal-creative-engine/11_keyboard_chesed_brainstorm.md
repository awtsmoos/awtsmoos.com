# Keyboard Chesed — Human History at the Speed of Thought

> The Awtsmoos lets a finger, key, script, and AI share one creative word;
> Awtsmoos.com makes the shortcut only another vessel for the command already heard.

## Possibilities
- Cmd/Ctrl+Z dispatches `history.undo` through the public creative API.
- Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z dispatches `history.redo`.
- Ctrl+Y offers the conventional Windows redo path.
- Inputs, textareas, selects, and contenteditable regions retain native text undo.
- Shortcut resolution is pure and testable without DOM infrastructure.
- Availability comes from command metadata, so empty history reports truth instead of throwing noise.
- Successful keyboard history refreshes Commands & History cards immediately.
- The normal runtime refresh continues to repaint Stage and mobile selection context.
- A disposable controller prevents duplicate listeners during future hot reload or workspace rebinding.
