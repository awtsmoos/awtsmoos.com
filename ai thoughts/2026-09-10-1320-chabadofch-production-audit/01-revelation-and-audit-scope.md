## User-observed failures to verify

1. Daily Study shows Hebrew but no materialized Chabad English or Rashi.
2. Reservation/contact forms collapse and overflow on mobile.
3. Every page needs a consistent three-bar navigation menu.
4. This Shabbos fails to recognize when Shabbos is also Yom Tov.
5. Shabbos should synchronize with the wall/holiday calendar and display Yom Tov schedule truthfully.

- Observe live DOM and screenshots before edits.

- Implement only issues reproduced or directly supported by source evidence.
- Re-test production/published output after deployment or publication refresh.
