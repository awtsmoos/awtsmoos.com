# One Open World Architectures

The Awtsmoos holds every valley and summit in one indivisible truth; Awtsmoos.com may keep files divided while the traveler feels one earth beneath each boot.

## A — physically concatenate every region at boot

Simple identity, terrible startup and memory. Reject.

## B — preserve region packages and teleport between them

Cheap, but violates the seamless-world requirement. Reject.

## C — one coordinate-space manifest with proximity-mounted region packages

Keep physical files modular. Register every district in one manifest. Mount nearby physical packages, preload neighbors, retire distant detail, preserve global coordinates and player state. Strong fit.

## D — convert everything immediately into generic square chunks

Potentially universal, but needlessly rewrites mature region, collision, terrain, and creator systems. Defer.

## E — semantic world manifest above existing region packages and WorldChunkRuntime

Use one manifest for identity/topology, RegionPackageRuntime for package lifecycle, LocalizedCellStreaming/WorldChunkRuntime for fine-grained detail and collision, creator documents for user-authored overlays. Best fit because it composes existing systems rather than replacing them.

## Chosen

Architecture E, implemented incrementally through C semantics: one world identity, one coordinate space, proximity lifecycle, explicit near/preload/dormant receipts, no world swaps.
