# Chapter 6

The corridor stretched before her like a throat. Mara pressed her back against cold stone, listening. Somewhere ahead, glass crunched under an unseen foot.

She had found it—the archive her mother had died to protect. Dust motes swam through shafts of pale light where the ceiling had cracked years ago. Shelves of faded ledgers lined the walls, their spines cracked and weeping binding glue.

A sound. Breathing.

She moved toward it, each step deliberate, her hand finding the knife at her belt. The archive keeper's desk sat empty. But on it lay a journal, its leather cover still warm.

Her fingers trembled as she opened it. Inside, her mother's handwriting. Dates she recognized. Names she had never seen.

And at the bottom of the final page, a name she knew too well.

Her brother's name.

The door behind her groaned shut. She was not alone.
