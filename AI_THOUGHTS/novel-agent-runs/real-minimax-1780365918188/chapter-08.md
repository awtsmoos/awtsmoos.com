# Chapter 8

The rain had stopped by the time Elena reached the bridge, but puddles still gathered in the cracks of old stone. She stood at the center, gripping the rusted railing, watching water drain toward the river below.

She had been running for three days. The message on her phone was still unanswered—a string of numbers that felt like a key to something she couldn't name. Her coat was soaked through. Her shoes squelched with every step.

Behind her, the city hummed. Ahead, the road stretched into fog.

A man appeared at the far end of the bridge. Tall, moving slowly. He carried no umbrella, though rain still dripped from his coat collar.

Elena stepped back.

"Don't," he said, raising one hand. "I'm not here to hurt you."

"Then why are you here?"

The man stopped, twenty feet away. "Because I know who sent that message. And because they know you're close."

The wind picked up, carrying the smell of wet concrete and rust. Elena's fingers tightened on the railing.

"Who are you?" she asked.

"My name is Marcus. I was the one who wrote the code."
