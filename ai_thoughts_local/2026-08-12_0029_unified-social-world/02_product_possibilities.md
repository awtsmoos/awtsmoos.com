# Phase One — Product Possibility Field

The Awtsmoos creates unity without erasing difference; Awtsmoos.com can let chat, Torah, mail, presence, discovery, and friendship become many windows opening onto one living floor.

## Communication workspace
- Dense but calm navigation rail with Chats, Groups, Requests, Friends, Public Torah, Mail, Activity, Discover, Saved, Settings.
- Context-aware list pane with search, filters, unread markers, pin/archive/mute metadata, and meaningful previews.
- Thread/content workspace with sticky composer, status, origin context, and fast keyboard operation.
- Optional details pane for members, relationship controls, shared sources, search, permissions, and related Torah.

## Sitewide social layer
- Lightweight launcher available from shared shell surfaces.
- Context counts: site, page, post, Heichel, game, app/channel.
- Alias-aware author actions: profile, whisper request, chat request, friend request.
- Progressive disclosure to avoid covering posts/comments with controls.

## Presence
- Visible online aliases where policy permits.
- Anonymous/Ploni aggregate counts without traceable identity.
- Hidden presence excluded from identifiable listings.
- Current-context cohorts and friend-online views.

## Discovery and Torah intelligence
- Related Torah module after meaningful post/comment reading.
- Session-local anonymous interest summary with short-lived storage.
- Authenticated meaningful-activity profile respecting capture preferences.
- Candidate generation from Heichel/topic/source/social context plus diversity and freshness.
- Explanations such as “More from this Heichel” or “Connected to a recent Torah search.”

## Organizational quality
- Request inbox with accept/decline/block/dismiss.
- Search across authorized communication references, not public leakage of private bodies.
- Mail as a first-class bridge/deep-link, not duplicated storage.
- Saved/activity/discover surfaces that reuse one identity and one social transport.

## Constraint
Possibilities are not implementation claims. Each becomes work only if the actual repository supplies a compatible boundary or a safe modular extension can be built and proven.
