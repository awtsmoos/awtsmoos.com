# Phase One — Unbounded Game-Suite Brainstorm

Awtsmoos gives every game a different garment; observation must precede imitation.

Ideal state: every playable route direct-loads, reaches interaction without artificial waits, has no console/network failures, fits portrait and landscape phones, exposes thumb-sized controls, keeps a stable 30-60 FPS render loop, handles resize/orientation, and supports start/restart/game-over without trapped overlays.

Census dimensions: HTTP status, ready time, console/resource failures, viewport overflow, canvas/WebGL size, visible loading/splash layers, control dimensions, animation-frame cadence, and discoverable start/play controls.

Possible repair families: responsive canvas shells, pointer normalization, nonblocking asset warm-up, stable RAF scheduling, visibility pausing, resize handling, deterministic restart, mechanics fixes, and accessible mobile menus.
