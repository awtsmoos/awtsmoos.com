# Phase Two — Improved Brainstorm

> Oros need Keilim. The Awtsmoos gives both: immense capability should arrive through simple forms.

Every surface must answer: Where am I? Who am I acting as? What can I do? What is happening? How do I recover?

Prefer small proven shared patterns: contextual header, async state, local action status, identity/destination badge, responsive pane, advanced-details disclosure, notification signal. Their implementation form follows existing architecture rather than a new framework.

Mail prioritizes auth clarity, scanning, thread readability, preserved context, and compose safety. Social prioritizes identity/destination clarity and conversation ancestry. Post creation prioritizes editor focus, draft safety, preview, and publish feedback. Heichel editing prioritizes outline/current-context/save safety. Missions prioritize meaningful signal hierarchy and direct safe action.

Visual grammar: structural whitespace, typography hierarchy, restrained pills, bounded reading width, thumb-reachable mobile actions, motion only for spatial explanation. Reliability grammar: intentional lazy/network fallbacks and preserved input.
