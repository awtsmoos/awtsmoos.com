# Business and AWS-Replacement Strategy

## Positioning

Do not market Awtsmoos primarily as an AWS clone. Replace the experience of needing AWS: "Describe it. Awtsmoos builds it, publishes it, hosts it, and keeps it running."

Most customers want a website/app online, not a collection of infrastructure knobs. ChatGPT is the simple interface; Geelooy OS reveals the full cloud for advanced users.

## Initial commercial wedge

Hosting is the first mass-market need. Sell outcomes immediately: business website creation, managed hosting, custom-domain setup, migrations, custom apps, agency hosting, and ongoing AI-assisted maintenance. Do not wait for every future cloud primitive before charging for real work that already functions.

## Revenue layers

- recurring hosting plans;
- usage/Perutas above included allowances;
- custom domains and DNS management;
- managed database/storage/compute/AI/email;
- professional setup and migration;
- agency/white-label plans;
- premium templates/assets/marketplace commission;
- priority support and enterprise contracts.

## Simple plan ladder

Free experimentation; Launch for one serious site; Creator for multiple creator projects; Business for operational websites/apps; Agency for many client sites; Scale for high traffic; Enterprise for contracts. Each plan presents understandable allowances first and detailed infrastructure units second.

## Million-dollar focus

Do not optimize for millions of users before product economics work. Roughly 1,701 customers at $49/month, 842 at $99/month, 279 agencies at $299/month, or 84 scale customers at $999/month each correspond to about $1M annual recurring revenue before churn/discounts. A mixed customer base is preferable.

## Margin law

Measure revenue minus compute, bandwidth, storage, database, AI, payment fees, support, abuse, and refunds per project/customer. Shared static/CDN/function/database infrastructure should serve small customers economically; dedicated compute is reserved for workloads that require it.

## Control-plane advantage

Awtsmoos may manage workloads on Awtsmoos infrastructure, connected customer machines, or external cloud/VPS providers through Tunnel. This allows a powerful control plane before owning enormous physical infrastructure and can eventually support placement/cost optimization.

## Product principle

Complexity should be available, not required. Every advanced console action corresponds to an API, and UI, Geelooy OS, Tunnel, CLI, and ChatGPT are clients of that same API truth.
