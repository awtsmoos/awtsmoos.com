# Posts and Series

Posts are content records; series provide organization and compatibility-sensitive hierarchy around them.

## Post flows

Heichel post routes support reading/creation, details, singular post behavior, submitted-post queues, approval/denial, sections/subsections/verses, translations, rich-post views, and comment trees.

## Series flows

Series support root/subseries trees, details/breadcrumbs, moving subseries, post membership/order changes, filters, translations, alternate groups, and compatibility aliases.

## Canonical evolution

New content APIs exist beside older series/post paths. Generated duplicates can be real compatibility entries, not extractor bugs.

## Safe mutation

Inspect both parent and target series rules plus ownership/editor authority before moving/deleting content.
