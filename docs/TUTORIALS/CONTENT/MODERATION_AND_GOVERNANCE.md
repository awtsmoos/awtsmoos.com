# Moderation and Governance

Social write workflows can move through submission/review/moderation rather than immediate publication.

## Observed systems

Heichel roles/editors/members, invites, submission settings, submitted posts/comments, review approval/rejection, moderation queues/actions/escalations/reports, and unified-social governance/review routes.

## Teaching rule

Do not treat POST as synonymous with “published now.” Follow the exact handler to see whether it creates canonical content, a pending submission, review record, moderation event, or another staged artifact.

## Authority

Review/approval actions need stronger resource authority than ordinary authenticated reads.
