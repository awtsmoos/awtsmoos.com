# Heichelos

A Heichel is a governed Social content/community space, not merely a folder.

## Observed capabilities

- create/read/update/delete Heichel behavior;
- ownership checks;
- editor/member/role management;
- submission settings;
- invites/review/moderation;
- post/question/content organization;
- series/subseries relationships;
- community/full settings and discovery.

## Authority

Login/API-key identity does not automatically grant Heichel mutation. Read owner/editor/member/moderation checks in the exact handler.

## Investigation

Start at `/api/social/heichelos/:heichel`, then follow its route factory imports and generated related-route links.
