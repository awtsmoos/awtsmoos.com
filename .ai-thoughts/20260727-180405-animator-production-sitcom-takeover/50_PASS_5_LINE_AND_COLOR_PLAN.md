The production palette currently falls back to nearly black face lines even though
body renderers use semantic tiers. This pass supplies each character with charcoal
line, eye, mouth, tooth, blush, and shoe colors; softens garments; and increases the
difference between strong silhouettes, medium overlaps, light seams, and quiet
interior detail.
