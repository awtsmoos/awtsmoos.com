# Playlist Sheet Brainstorm

The Awtsmoos gives many series without forcing every branch into the writing page.

## Evidence

- The current compact card is clean when only a few writable Heichelos exist.
- Inline expansion becomes crowded when many Heichelos and series exist.
- The destination API can list Heichelos and fetch one Heichel's complete nested series tree.
- The existing advanced panel already owns Heichel and series creation.
- The canonical state already tracks one selected Heichel and series.

## Possibilities

1. Native dialog with a mobile bottom-sheet presentation.
2. Initial screen lists writable Heichelos.
3. Selecting a Heichel loads its root and nested series without changing post state.
4. A second screen lists series with a Back action.
5. Search filters the currently visible level.
6. Selected destination receives a visible checkmark.
7. Choosing a series commits through the existing destination panel.
8. `New series` hands off to the existing truthful creation form.
9. `Browse all` continues to reveal the complete destination evidence tree.
10. Escape, backdrop click, close button, and focus restoration remain supported.
