## Remaining Head Debt

This family is accepted only as a stable reusable foundation, not as final reference fidelity.

- Male kippahs remain visually dark and slightly too dominant against the brown crown.
- Male temple joins still need softer integration after beard cheek roots are corrected.
- Miriam’s wrap remains darker and broader than the reference.
- Miriam’s sweep still needs subtle strand asymmetry and softer cloth contact after face proportions and brows are refined.
- Peyot remain wire-like and must be revisited with beard and ear relationships.

## Handoff Decision

The beard masks and mouth apertures now dominate identity failure more strongly than the remaining crown defects. Continue to beard-root, jaw-flow, moustache, expression-mouth, teeth, tongue, and phoneme deformation work. Return to crown contrast and temple integration after the face no longer reads as a mask.
